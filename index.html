How it works

No endless catalog and no page-one ads. A conversation that ends in the best offer from the brands that actually solve your problem.

1

Describe the problem

"My dog destroys every toy." The Concierge asks up to 3 quick questions, shown as tappable chips.

2

Get a plan + shortlist

A step-by-step fix and ~3 products, with "why this" explanations. Browsing stays limited to this problem.

3

Vendors compete live

Shortlisted vendors send private offers. Tap "Ask for a better deal". Vendors can ask for a few minutes. It all happens alongside the chat.

4

Play & win credits

Short skill games, paid for by ads, earn store credits that are applied automatically at checkout.

Buzz Feed: social proof that travels

One social feed in the Shopper, Vendor and Admin portals. Real reviews and stories, labelled brand promotions and contextual banner ads, with moderation built in.

Shoppers

Post & share

Reviews with star ratings and a Verified purchase badge, stories, tips and questions. Share to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, or copy a link.

Vendors

Promote & reply

Sponsored promotions with budgets and targeting (reviewed by eBuzz), brand replies to reviews, and a view of every mention.

Admin

Moderate & monetise

AI plus human moderation, promotion ad review, and banner ads from Google Ad Manager and Microsoft Advertising, contextual-only and capped.

Clickable prototype

Every portal is chat-first: a copilot in the centre, interactive components inside the conversation, and a context panel with only what matters right now.

Validation, in brief

The model works, and it stands out if we build on the one thing giants won't copy: live competition at the shortlist moment.

Keep & invest

The Deal Room is the moat

Chat discovery is becoming a commodity (Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity). Live multi-vendor offers aren't, and they create price-elasticity data no one else has.

Change

Design for law & trust

Session-scoped, anonymised "price to beat" (antitrust). Skill-based, free-to-play games with published odds (sweepstakes law, EU Digital Fairness Act). Agents negotiate instantly, which fixes Priceline's old "bidding hassle".

Add

More ways to win

Start in one vertical (Home, Sleep & WFH Wellness). Brand-sponsored game levels. Deal Boost + vendor SaaS. Offer eBuzz deals to other AI agents through the UCP/ACP protocols.

Competitive positioning

Local-first, private by design

Every portal keeps its data in a SQLite database on the device and syncs it across devices. Personal and financial details are encrypted on the device, so the central database stores only what the platform strictly needs.

This device only

Memory & conversations

What the Concierge remembers about you never leaves your device.

E2EE vault

Name, email, address, phone

Encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM) and synced as ciphertext. Only your own devices can read it. Admins see a pseudonymous ID.

Central

Only what's required

Order amounts for settlement, state for sales tax, the credit ledger for fraud checks, and roles for access control. Card numbers stay with the payment processor.

Try it: open any portal, click the sync chip in the top bar, then choose "Open as Phone" to watch a second device sync.

An AI-first company

26 agents run every operational process. Humans approve anything that publishes content, signs contracts or moves money.
